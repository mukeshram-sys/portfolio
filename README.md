## Features & UX
- **Living Animation Background**: The 238-frame image sequence runs continuously in the background as you scroll through the portfolio.
- **Narrative Section Cross-fade**: As you scroll through the page, sections smoothly glide and cross-fade in sync with the model animation.
- **Floating Dot Navigation**: Clickable indicator on the right edge allowing 1-click smooth jump to any of the 6 sections.
- **Ultra-Smooth Interpolation (Lerp)**: Linear interpolation momentum loop (`LERP_FACTOR = 0.08`) delivers silky 60–120Hz smooth scrolling without discrete mouse-wheel stepping.
- **Glassmorphism & Legibility**: Translucent glass cards with soft radial scrims guarantee 100% legibility against all animation frames.
